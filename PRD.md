# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Name
Lagan Portfolio

## 2. Product Overview
This portfolio is a personal website for Lagan that introduces the developer, highlights learning interests, and provides a way to contact them.

## 3. Objectives
- Present a professional yet personal brand
- Show key information in a polished layout
- Make the site responsive and easy to view on all devices
- Help visitors understand the user’s interests and goals

## 4. Target Audience
- Recruiters
- Potential collaborators
- Peers and friends
- General visitors to the portfolio

## 5. User Needs
- Learn who the person is
- Understand their goals and interests
- View their portfolio in a visually engaging format
- Contact them through email

## 6. Functional Requirements
1. The website must display a clear personalized introduction.
2. The page must include an About Me section.
3. The page must include a Projects or Ideas section.
4. The page must include an Interests section.
5. The page must include a Contact section with email details.
6. The site must be responsive across mobile and desktop layouts.
7. The footer must show the creator’s name and the current year.

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements
- Simple static implementation
- Fast load speed
- Readable typography and accessible structure
- Easy to maintain in the future

## 8. UX Requirements
- Friendly, modern, and welcoming visual style
- Clear hierarchy of information
- Sharp contrast for readability
- Calls to action that guide users to connect

## 9. Acceptance Criteria
- A visitor can open the portfolio and identify the owner quickly.
- The site includes all key sections outlined above.
- The layout remains usable on smaller screens.
- The contact email is easy to find.
- The site feels polished and personalized.
